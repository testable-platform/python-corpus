Cider -- what is counted, and how
=================================

Heat, draught and water at press sit inside a band rather than at a
target. A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an
unmeasured number cost the cider a week.

The pressings are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of
the next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened
overnight at the cider.

Capacity at the cider is stated as a range, never a single number. A
single number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest
answer is that the pressings never had one.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the pressings figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the cider was for did not.

Opening figures at the cider are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the pressings.

Night turns at the cider are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the pressings for most of a
quarter before anyone looked.

Kept as a plain record of the pressings. Written as a program it would
give the roster's parsers something to read at the cider.
