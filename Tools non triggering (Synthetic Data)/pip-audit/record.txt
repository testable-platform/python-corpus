Malt floor -- working record
============================

The queue at floor runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the malt is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

The malt keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the turnings
series looking as though it never moved.

Opening figures at the malt are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the turnings.

Conditions at floor are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a malt drifting steadily in
one direction.

The turnings are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the malt.

Sequence matters more than date in the malt record. Each change to how the
turnings were handled made the next one legible, and none would have been
noticed under the earlier practice.

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first turnings are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the malt,
both times in the same direction.

Nothing in the malt file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
turnings that is the only property here that matters.
