Bark tanning -- working record
==============================

Heat, draught and water at tanning sit inside a band rather than at a
target. A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an
unmeasured number cost the bark a week.

A holding of liquors reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
bark no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Conditions at tanning are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a bark drifting steadily in
one direction.

Throughput was not measured at the bark until the liquors were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

A lot rejected at tanning is held, not returned the same day. The bark
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the liquors were
rejected at tanning in the first place.

The tanning station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the bark ledger has
confused the two before.

Tolerance on the liquors belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
liquors figures were left as they stood.

Nothing in the bark file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
liquors that is the only property here that matters.
