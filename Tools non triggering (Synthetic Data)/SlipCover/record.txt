Record of fulling at the wool
=============================

The queue at fulling runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the wool is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

The cycles are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the wool.

Loss between receipt and the close of fulling is attributed, not written
off. The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is
the one step the wool never skips.

Heat, draught and water at fulling sit inside a band rather than at a
target. A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an
unmeasured number cost the wool a week.

Who counted the cycles is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the wool, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the wool this is understood to be the weak
point of the whole arrangement for the cycles.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the cycles figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the wool was for did not.

The cycles are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the wool tracks is derived from the cycles,
and each derivation is written down.

Kept as a plain record of the cycles. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the wool.
