Osier: standing notes on the frames
===================================

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the osier is raised,
not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at weaving, where it
costs a turn instead of an hour.

Loss between receipt and the close of weaving is attributed, not written
off. The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is
the one step the osier never skips.

Throughput was not measured at the osier until the frames were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

When the frames were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the osier
eventually explained it anyway.

Corrections are appended at the osier, never edited in place. The history
of the frames figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

Conditions at weaving are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a osier drifting steadily in
one direction.

A record of the osier, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose of
the folder these frames notes sit in.
