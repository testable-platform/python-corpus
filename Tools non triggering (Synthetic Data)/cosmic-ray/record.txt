Record of axle at the cartwright
================================

Opening figures at the cartwright are carried forward, not re-entered. A
re-entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the sets.

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the cartwright is
raised, not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at axle, where
it costs a turn instead of an hour.

Corrections are appended at the cartwright, never edited in place. The
history of the sets figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

When the sets were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the
cartwright eventually explained it anyway.

Heat, draught and water at axle sit inside a band rather than at a target.
A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an unmeasured
number cost the cartwright a week.

A holding of sets reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
cartwright no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence,
having done so for most of one season.

A lot rejected at axle is held, not returned the same day. The cartwright
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the sets were
rejected at axle in the first place.

Nothing in the cartwright file executes and nothing declares a project.
For the sets that is the only property here that matters.
