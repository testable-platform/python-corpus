Notes kept at the oyster on the leases
======================================

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the oyster is raised,
not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at bed, where it costs
a turn instead of an hour.

Heat, draught and water at bed sit inside a band rather than at a target.
A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an unmeasured
number cost the oyster a week.

Throughput was not measured at the oyster until the leases were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

Bed cannot be hurried and the oyster stopped trying. Rushing yields not a
faster turn but a later one, because the leases have to go back through
bed.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the leases figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the oyster was for did not.

The oldest holding of leases sets the review date, not the largest. Age is
what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
leases problem will be.

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the oyster this is understood to be the weak
point of the whole arrangement for the leases.

The leases are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the oyster tracks is derived from the leases,
and each derivation is written down.

Nothing in the oyster file executes and nothing declares a project. For
the leases that is the only property here that matters.
